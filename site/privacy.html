YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Unsolved.fyi connects community needs with people who may be able to help. This page explains how information is handled when you browse, share a need, or offer help.

Submissions and offers to help

When you send a form, your name, email address, and the details you enter are sent through FormSubmit to problems@unsolved.fyi. Offers also include the need’s ID, title, and link. We use this information to review needs, respond, and arrange next steps. Please avoid including sensitive personal information or details about other people without their permission.

Submissions are reviewed manually before publication. Your email address is for follow-up and is not automatically published. We ask before sharing your contact details with others. FormSubmit processes these submissions under its privacy policy.

Public listings

Published need descriptions, locations, verification information, and source links are publicly accessible and downloadable. Contact us if a listing includes personal information you want reviewed or removed.

Optional Google Analytics

If analytics is configured, we ask for your permission before loading Google Analytics or setting its cookies. You can decline and continue using the site. If you allow it, Google receives usage information such as page visits, browser and device information, and network information needed to process the request. Analytics cookies help distinguish visits.

Our analytics hook sends page views without URL query strings or fragments and does not send form contents. Advertising signals and ad personalization are disabled in the hook. Google processes analytics data under its privacy policy; learn how Google uses information from partner sites.

Use to change your choice. Declining stops future analytics from this site; it does not erase information already received by Google or automatically remove existing cookies. You can remove those cookies in your browser settings.

Browser storage and other services

We store your theme preference and analytics choice in your browser’s local storage. If storage is unavailable, your choices may not persist. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives network information such as your IP address and browser details when fonts are requested. Our hosting provider may process request information to deliver and protect the site. External source links lead to sites with their own privacy practices.

Questions and requests

Email problems@unsolved.fyi to ask about your information or request correction or deletion of submissions or public personal details. Include enough information to identify the relevant submission or listing. We may need to verify your request and coordinate with service providers.
